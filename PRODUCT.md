# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Fans**: mobile-first, browsing in private leisure moments (evening, in bed). Job: discover creators, subscribe, unlock posts, tip. Mood: intimacy, anticipation, discretion.
- **Creators**: manage their studio (posts, earnings, tips, ambassador program) on desktop or mobile. Job: publish, track revenue, grow. Mood: feeling professional and in control of a real business.
- **Admins**: internal operations (KYC, commissions, referrals). Pure utility.

## Product Purpose

GetFanSee is a creator subscription and tipping platform (18+ content). Fans pay to access creator content via subscriptions, pay-per-view unlocks, and tips. Success = fans feel a premium, discreet, intimate experience; creators feel trusted with their money.

## Brand Personality

Warm, intimate, quietly luxurious. Three words: **sensual, restrained, premium**. The feel of a high-end intimate-wellness brand (reference: LELO.com) — elegance and suggestion, never loud or vulgar. Fashion-editorial confidence with generous whitespace. The creator's imagery is the hero; the UI recedes (reference: Instagram's content-first restraint).

## Anti-references

- **The current v3 design system itself**: Violet #8B5CF6 × Gold #F59E0B gradients, neon glows, decorative glassmorphism — reads as generic AI output.
- AI-slop patterns: purple/indigo gradient buttons, gradient text, glow shadows, identical icon-card grids, hard colored side-stripes on cards/alerts.
- Garish cam-site aesthetics: saturated pink/purple on black, badge overload, flashing CTAs.
- Dense dashboard cramming on fan-facing surfaces.

## Design Principles

1. **Content is the luxury.** Creator imagery carries the sensuality; chrome stays near-monochrome and minimal. Color appears rarely and deliberately.
2. **Whitespace is the brand.** Generous spacing and breathing room signal premium. When in doubt, remove an element rather than shrink it.
3. **Suggest, don't shout.** Warmth and intimacy come from tone, typography, and soft surfaces — not from loud accent colors or glow effects.
4. **Money deserves clarity.** Wallet, tips, earnings: precise typography, calm hierarchy, zero ambiguity. Trust is part of seduction.
5. **One palette everywhere.** Every surface (fan, creator, admin, legal) draws from the same token set. No page invents its own colors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1 on dark backgrounds (dark UI makes muted grays fail easily — verify, don't assume).
- `prefers-reduced-motion` honored on all animations.
- Touch targets ≥44px on mobile (fan surfaces are mobile-first).
- Age gate and discretion considerations: avoid explicit imagery in system UI itself.
